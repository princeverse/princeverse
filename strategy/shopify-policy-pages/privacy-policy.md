# Privacy Policy — paste into Shopify Settings → Policies → Privacy policy

> ⚠️ Template draft, not legal advice. Shopify can auto-generate a base
> privacy policy for you (Settings → Policies → "Create from template")
> which is often better-maintained for compliance — use that as the base
> and layer in the specifics below. If you'll have EU/UK/California
> customers in meaningful volume, have a lawyer review for GDPR/CCPA
> compliance specifically.

---

**What we collect**

When you make a purchase, we collect your name, email address, billing
address, and payment information (processed securely by our payment
provider — we never store your full card details ourselves).

**How we use it**

- To fulfill your order and deliver your digital files
- To send order confirmations and download links
- To respond to customer service requests
- With your consent, to send occasional emails about new products (you
  can unsubscribe anytime)

**Third parties we share data with**

- Our payment processor (Shopify Payments / PayPal), to process your
  transaction
- Shopify, our e-commerce platform, which stores order and account data
- Analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics), to understand site traffic —
  this data is aggregated and not used to identify you individually

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

**Your rights**

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct
it, or ask us to delete it (subject to what we're legally required to
keep for tax/accounting purposes) by emailing us.

**Cookies**

This site uses cookies to keep your cart working and to understand how
visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser, though
some site features may not work correctly without them.

**Contact**

Questions about this policy: [your business email]
