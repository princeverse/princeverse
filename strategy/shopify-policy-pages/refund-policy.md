**Digital Products**

All items sold in this shop are digital downloads delivered instantly
after purchase. Because the product is delivered immediately and cannot
be "returned," **all sales are final** — we do not offer refunds or
exchanges for change of mind.

**When we will help**

We will issue a refund or replacement file if:
- The file is corrupted, doesn't open, or doesn't match the listing
  description
- You were charged more than once for the same order
- The wrong file was delivered

To request this, email us within 7 days of purchase with your order
number and a description of the issue. We'll respond within 2 business
days and either send a corrected file or issue a refund.

**Please note:** because our products are templates you customize
yourself (printables, planners), we cannot offer refunds for reasons such
as "I didn't like the design" or "I don't need it anymore" once the file
has been downloaded — please review the listing photos and description
carefully before purchasing, or message us with questions first.
