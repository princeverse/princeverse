100 prompts. Every turn, pick Truth or Temptation. Answer honestly or take the dare — either way, the night gets warmer with every round.

Suggested Setup

  • Two drinks, low light, phones face-down except this one.
  • Sit close enough to touch — a couch or the edge of a bed works better than a table.
  • Give yourselves at least 30 uninterrupted minutes. This isn't a game to rush.

How to Play

  • Each turn, one partner chooses Truth or Temptation. Alternate who chooses each round.
  • Truth: answer the prompt honestly, out loud.
  • Temptation: do the dare. Every dare is optional and can be swapped for a Truth instead if you'd rather.
  • You can skip any prompt entirely, no explanation needed — you'll just draw a different one.
  • The game escalates automatically in three stages, ending at "After 9" — the boldest tier.
  • Scoring is optional: Temptation is worth 2 points, Truth is worth 1, tracked together as one shared score, not partner vs. partner.
