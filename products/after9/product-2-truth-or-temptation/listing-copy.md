## FAQ
**Is this the same as Product 1 (How Well Do You Really Know Me)?**
No — that game is a shared question set you both answer together. This
one is choice-driven: every turn, you pick Truth or Temptation, and the
game plays differently depending what you choose.

**What if we don't want the dares to get too bold?**
Every Temptation can be swapped for a Truth instead — you're always in
control of how far it goes.

**Is scoring required?**
No, it's fully optional and can be turned on from the start screen.

**Do we need anything besides our phone?**
No extra pieces — the app includes a suggested setup, but you can play
with nothing but the device you're reading this on.
