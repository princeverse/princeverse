Really Know Me?
A prediction game, not just a conversation.
One of you answers honestly and privately. The other predicts it. Then you reveal — and find out how well you really know each other, one round at a time.

How It Works

  • Each round, one partner privately locks in a real answer, then hands the phone over.
  • The other partner predicts what was chosen — then you reveal together.
  • You'll alternate who answers and who predicts each turn.
  • Nine questions per round, drawn from a larger pool — replay and you'll see new ones.
  • Skip any question, any time, no explanation needed.
  • Four rounds, each a little more revealing than the last — ending at After.9.
This is collaborative, not competitive — the score is something you build together.
