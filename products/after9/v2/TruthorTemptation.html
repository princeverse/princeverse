100 prompts. Every turn, pick Truth or Temptation. Answer honestly or take the invitation — either way, the night gets warmer with every round.

Suggested Setup

  • Two drinks, low light, phones face-down except this one.
  • Sit close enough to touch — a couch or the edge of a bed works better than a table.
  • Give yourselves at least 30 uninterrupted minutes.

How to Play

  • Each turn, one partner chooses Truth or Temptation. Alternate who chooses.
  • Truth: answer the prompt honestly, out loud.
  • Temptation: an invitation to try something together — never an instruction either of you has to follow.
  • Every prompt can be skipped, for any reason, with no explanation needed.
  • A Temptation can always be exchanged for a Truth instead.
  • The game escalates automatically in three stages, ending at "After.9" — the warmest tier.
  • Scoring is optional: Temptation is worth 2 points, Truth is worth 1, tracked together as one shared score.
Everything here is optional. Either of you can pause, change course, or stop at any point — silence or hesitation is never a yes.
