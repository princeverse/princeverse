How To Use This Guide

Who it's for, and how the categories fit together
Who this is for

Founders, marketers, and sales teams who keep seeing new "AI sales tool" ads and want a clear map before they buy anything. This guide organizes the crowded AI go-to-market (GTM) space into five plain-English categories, gives real examples of tools in each one, and shows how they connect into a single working stack.

This is an educational buyer's guide, not a paid promotion. Tool names, pricing tiers, and features are included as examples to illustrate each category — always confirm current pricing and features on the vendor's own site before buying, since SaaS pricing and plans change often.
