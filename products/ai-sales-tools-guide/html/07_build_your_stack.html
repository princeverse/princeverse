Build Your Stack

Three starter stacks by team size and budget
Solo founder / pre-seed
1 visitor ID tool with a free plan (Customers.ai or RB2B) to see who's already on your site
1 enrichment tool with a free tier (Clay's free plan or Apollo's free plan) for list-building
Skip the AI SDR platform until you're sending 500+ outbound touches/month — write sequences yourself first
Small team, 2–10 people
Visitor ID + intent tool (Warmly or Customers.ai) feeding your CRM
Clay or Apollo for enrichment, paired with Instantly or Smartlead for send infrastructure
Reply.io or AiSDR to draft and manage sequences once volume outgrows manual sending
Growing team, agency, or high call volume
Full waterfall enrichment in Clay feeding a dedicated cold email infra tool
AiSDR or a similar AI SDR platform running the bulk of first-touch outbound
A voice AI layer (Bland AI, Retell AI, or Uplift AI for multilingual needs) for qualification calls and follow-up
Before you buy anything
#Question to ask the vendor
1What happens to my data if I cancel? Can I export contacts and sequences?
2Is pricing per-seat, per-credit, or flat — and what triggers an overage charge?
3Does this tool integrate directly with my CRM, or does it need a middle layer (Zapier/Make)?
4What's the actual free-trial length, and is a card required to start it?
