Website Visitor ID & Intent Data

Turning anonymous traffic into named leads

Most website visitors leave without filling out a form. This category matches anonymous traffic against identity and business databases to reveal who was on your site, what they looked at, and how to reach them — so your team can follow up while intent is still warm instead of waiting for an inbound form fill.

Customers.aiVisitor ID
Identifies known visitors on your website (name, title, company, contact details where available) and can auto-trigger AI-drafted outbound email once a visitor matches your ideal customer profile. Popular with e-commerce and B2B teams that want to act on site traffic without building anything custom.
Best for: solo founders – mid-size teamsStarting tier: free plan + paid tiers from ~$100s/mo
RB2BVisitor ID
A LinkedIn-focused visitor identification tool built for B2B — surfaces the individual (not just the company) behind a site visit and pushes alerts straight into Slack, so reps can reach out same-day.
Best for: B2B SaaS, small sales teamsStarting tier: free plan available
WarmlyVisitor ID + Orchestration
Combines visitor identification with intent signals from your CRM and outbound tools, then auto-plays that data into Slack alerts, sequences, or a live chat widget when a high-fit account is on your site.
Best for: teams already running CRM + outbound toolsStarting tier: free plan + paid tiers
Leadfeeder / DealfrontVisitor ID
One of the earlier company-level website visitor tracking tools, now part of Dealfront. Identifies the company (not always the individual) visiting your site and scores accounts by engagement.
Best for: account-based marketing teamsStarting tier: paid plans, no perpetual free tier
Buyer's note: person-level identification accuracy varies by vendor and by country's privacy rules (GDPR/CCPA affect what can legally be matched). Ask any vendor directly what data source they use and how match rates are calculated before committing to an annual plan.
