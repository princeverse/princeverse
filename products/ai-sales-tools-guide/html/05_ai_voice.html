AI Voice & Conversational Sales

When the follow-up needs an actual conversation

Email and LinkedIn sequences only go so far — some qualification and support conversations still need a live back-and-forth. This category covers the voice AI infrastructure and agent platforms teams use to build automated phone or voice-chat experiences, from qualifying inbound calls to multilingual support.

Uplift AIVoice AI Infrastructure
A voice AI platform specializing in natural-sounding, low-latency text-to-speech and voice agents for South Asian languages (including Urdu and Hindi). Sales and support teams use it as the voice layer underneath a conversational agent when they need a multilingual, regionally accurate voice rather than a generic English-only one.
Best for: teams needing South Asian language voice agentsStarting tier: usage-based API pricing
Bland AIAI Phone Agents
A developer-focused platform for building AI phone agents that can make or take calls at scale — used for outbound qualification calls, appointment reminders, and inbound call handling.
Best for: teams building custom outbound/inbound call flowsStarting tier: usage-based (per-minute) pricing
Retell AIVoice Agent Platform
A platform for building and deploying voice AI agents with low-latency conversation handling, aimed at teams that want to prototype a voice agent quickly and plug it into their existing phone or CRM stack.
Best for: product/eng teams prototyping voice agentsStarting tier: usage-based API pricing
Buyer's note: voice AI is the newest and fastest-moving category in this guide. Features, language coverage, and pricing models change quickly — test with a real call script and real accents/languages from your audience before rolling out to production.
