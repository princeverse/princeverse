AI SDRs & Outbound Sequencing

Writing, sending, and following up at scale

This is the category most people picture first when they hear "AI sales tool": software that drafts personalized outbound messages, sends them across email and other channels, handles replies, and books meetings — either alongside a human SDR team or in place of an early one.

AiSDRAI SDR
Positioned as a full "virtual SDR": it researches leads, writes personalized outbound sequences in your brand voice, replies to prospects, handles objections in-thread, and books meetings straight onto reps' calendars.
Best for: teams without a full SDR headcount yetStarting tier: flat monthly plans (~$1,000+/mo range)
Reply.ioSales Engagement
A multichannel sales engagement platform (email, LinkedIn, calls, SMS) with AI-assisted sequence writing and an AI SDR add-on for teams that want automation layered onto a tool they already run their outbound through.
Best for: teams already doing manual outbound, ready to add AIStarting tier: paid plans from ~$50–$100+/seat/mo
InstantlyCold Email Infrastructure
Focused on cold email deliverability at scale — mailbox warm-up, sending infrastructure, and inbox rotation — plus a lead database and campaign builder. Often paired with an enrichment tool like Clay for the list itself.
Best for: high-volume cold email sendersStarting tier: paid plans from ~$30–$100+/mo
SmartleadCold Email Infrastructure
Similar niche to Instantly — unlimited mailbox sending accounts, warm-up, and deliverability monitoring built for agencies and teams running outbound at scale across many domains.
Best for: agencies running outbound for multiple clientsStarting tier: paid plans from ~$30–$100+/mo
Buyer's note: cold email deliverability depends on domain reputation and compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR opt-out rules) as much as the tool itself. No AI outbound tool can fix a burned sending domain — budget for proper domain warm-up regardless of which platform you choose.
