Data Enrichment & Waterfalls

Filling in the gaps on any lead list

No single data provider has every email or phone number. "Waterfall" enrichment tools query multiple providers in sequence (falling through to the next one when the first misses) so you get the highest match rate for the lowest cost per verified contact. This is the layer that makes outbound lists actually usable.

ClayEnrichment + Workflows
A spreadsheet-style workspace that runs data through 50+ providers in a waterfall, plus AI columns that can research a company, write a personalized line, or check a signal before it ever reaches a rep. Popular with growth and RevOps teams for building fully custom outbound workflows without engineering help.
Best for: growth/RevOps teams building custom workflowsStarting tier: free plan + paid tiers from ~$100+/mo
Apollo.ioEnrichment + Database
A large B2B contact database combined with built-in enrichment, email sequencing, and a dialer — an all-in-one option for teams that want prospecting and outreach in one login rather than stitching tools together.
Best for: teams wanting an all-in-one platformStarting tier: free plan + paid tiers
Clearbit (part of HubSpot Breeze)Enrichment
Firmographic and technographic enrichment now built into HubSpot's Breeze Intelligence — auto-fills company size, industry, and tech stack fields on form-fill or CRM record creation.
Best for: existing HubSpot usersStarting tier: bundled with HubSpot plans
ZoomInfoEnrichment + Database
One of the largest and most established B2B contact and intent-data providers, with deep org-chart and buying-committee data. Typically priced for mid-market and enterprise budgets rather than solo founders.
Best for: mid-market & enterprise sales teamsStarting tier: annual contracts, higher price point
