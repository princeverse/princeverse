# Product Notes — Kamasutra Collection

This is the first product in a new line, structured around the Kamasutra as a
framework rather than a single game mechanic. Same delivery model and brand
craft as After.9 (self-contained HTML, dark wine/gold/cream aesthetic,
tasteful-never-graphic content standard), positioned as its own collection so
it can grow independently — a guide first, with room for a quiz/match product
and others to follow inside the same structure later.

---

## The Kamasutra Guide

**Product summary**
A browsable, filterable reference guide — not a game. 36 positions organized
into three chapters (Foundations, Deepening, Uncharted), each with an original
name, a short tag line, a full descriptive entry, and a comfort tip. Filter by
energy level, flexibility required, and experience level, or hit "Surprise Me"
for a random pick. Favorite entries save locally for quick return visits.

**Target audience**
Same core After.9 audience (adults 21–40, dating or partnered) plus a slightly
broader pull — this format also appeals to couples who want a reference to
browse together or separately, not just a shared-session game night.

**Structure**
| Chapter | Positions | Energy | Flexibility | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations | 12 | Gentle | Low | Beginner |
| Deepening | 12 | Moderate | Medium | Intermediate |
| Uncharted | 12 | Intense | High | Advanced |

Every entry carries all three tags independently (a Deepening-chapter entry
might still be Low flexibility, etc.) — the chapter groups them for browsing,
but the filter bar reads the real tags, not just the chapter.

**Content standard**
Same house rule as the rest of the collection: descriptive, instructional,
never graphic or anatomical. Entries describe body positioning, support
(pillows, furniture), pacing, and connection (eye contact, hand placement) —
the same register used by mainstream published wellness/relationship guides,
not explicit material. Names are original to this product (not claimed as
literal Sanskrit translations) with a familiar alternate name noted in
parentheses where one exists, for recognizability.

**Expected use time**
Not session-bound like a game — designed for repeat browsing. A first pass
through one chapter runs 5–10 minutes; most value comes from returning to it
over time, which is why favorites/bookmarking matters more here than in the
game products.

**Number of entries**
36 positions, 12 per chapter — see the app's own count for the exact split by
energy/flexibility/experience once built.

**Replayability / return value**
Not a "no-repeat" mechanic like the games — this is a reference, so the whole
point is being able to return to the same or a new entry anytime. Favorites +
"Surprise Me" are the two mechanics designed to pull people back in.

**Recommended price (CAD)**
**$19–24 CAD**, same flagship tier as Truth or Temptation. Recommend launching
at **$22 CAD** — positioned above Truth or Temptation (broader reference
value, larger content library) but below where a "premium" repricing would
sit once it has reviews.

**Suggested Shopify title**
The Kamasutra Guide | After.9 Collection

**Suggested product subtitle**
36 positions. Filter by energy, flexibility, and experience.

**Five key benefits**
1. A real reference, not a card list — filter by energy, flexibility, or
   experience level to find what fits tonight
2. Favorite entries save automatically for quick return visits
3. "Surprise Me" turns browsing into its own small game
4. Every entry is written for comfort and connection, never a how-to in a
   clinical or graphic register
5. Instant access, works on any phone or tablet, no install required, works
   offline after first load

**What the buyer receives**
One self-contained HTML file (`TheKamasutraGuide.html`) — same delivery model
as the rest of the collection: opens directly in any modern browser, works
offline after first load, no account or login.

**Suggested product-page FAQ**
- *Is this explicit or graphic?* No — every entry is written descriptively,
  never anatomically or graphically. It reads like a guide, not adult content.
- *Do the names come from the real Kamasutra?* The structure and spirit are
  inspired by it; the entry names in this guide are original to keep the
  content clear and consistent, with a familiar alternate name noted where
  one exists.
- *Is this a game like your other products?* No — this is a browsable
  reference. Filter it, favorite what you like, come back to it anytime.
- *Does it save my favorites if I close the app?* Yes — favorites are saved
  on your device automatically.
