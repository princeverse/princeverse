# Refund Policy — After 9

> Template draft, not legal advice. Adjust for your jurisdiction before scaling revenue meaningfully.

---

All products sold by After 9 are digital downloads and interactive
digital experiences delivered instantly after purchase. Because access is
granted immediately, **all sales are final** — we do not offer refunds
for change of mind once a product has been accessed.

**When we will help**

We'll issue a refund or fix if:
- Your file/game doesn't open or load correctly
- You were charged more than once for the same order
- You received the wrong product

Email us within 7 days of purchase with your order number and a
description of the issue. We respond within 2 business days.

**Please note:** because our products are complete the moment they're
opened — no physical component, nothing to "return" — refunds can't be
issued for reasons like "we didn't enjoy it" or "it wasn't what we
expected." Every product page includes a clear description and how-to-play
explanation before you buy — please review it first, or message us with
questions.
