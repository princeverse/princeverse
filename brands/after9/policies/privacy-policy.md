**What we collect**

When you make a purchase, we collect your name, email address, billing
details, and payment information (processed securely by our payment
provider — we never store full card details ourselves).

**How we use it**

- To deliver your purchase and send order confirmations
- To respond to customer service requests
- With your consent, to send occasional emails about new releases (you
  can unsubscribe anytime)

**The nature of our products**

After 9 sells tasteful, adult-oriented couples' entertainment products —
games and prompts intended for consenting adult partners. We do not
collect, store, or have visibility into your answers, responses, or how
you use any product after purchase. Gameplay happens entirely on your own
device; nothing you do while playing is transmitted to us.

**Third parties we share data with**

- Our payment processor, to process transactions
- Shopify, our e-commerce platform, which stores order and account data
- Analytics tools, to understand aggregate site traffic — not to identify
  you individually

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

**Your rights**

You can request a copy of the data we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask
us to delete it (subject to records we're legally required to keep) by
emailing us.

**Cookies**

This site uses cookies to keep your cart working and understand traffic
patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features
may not work correctly without them.
